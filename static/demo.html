How it works

Nothing about your phone or number changes.

  1. A customer calls and nobody can pick up — lunch rush, closed, hands full.
  2. The unanswered call forwards automatically to the AI line.
  3. The AI answers in English or Chinese, takes the pickup order, and reads it back to confirm.
  4. The ticket is texted to your phone. Customer picks up as usual.
It only answers calls you were already missing — your phone always rings first, and you can switch it off yourself anytime by dialing a code.
